JOBS ERA SEO SETUP

1. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your purchased domain.
2. Upload the complete JobsEra folder contents to your hosting public_html/root folder.
3. Add your domain property in Google Search Console.
4. Submit: https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/sitemap.xml
5. For strong blog/job SEO, move production data from localStorage to a real backend and generate a unique crawlable URL for every post.
6. Use unique page titles and descriptions for every article/job.
7. Add JobPosting structured data only for genuine, active job listings.
8. Keep expired jobs updated or removed.
